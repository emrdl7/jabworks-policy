Our Last Camp — Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 October 2026

Operator: jabworks / Contact: cs@footer.kr

1. Information in the game

The game is free. No registration or login is required, and it does not send personal information or gameplay analytics to a developer-operated server. Progress, settings and gameplay records are stored on your device. There are no in-app purchases or custom cloud synchronization features. A rewarded ad is shown only when you choose it after collapsing (section 3).

2. Storage and deletion

Local data supports gameplay and continuing your progress. You can delete it using your device's app-data controls. Any operating-system backup and restore follows your device settings and the relevant provider's policies.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

If you choose "Watch an ad to get up now", a Google AdMob rewarded ad is shown. To serve and measure ads, Google may process information such as your advertising ID, IP address and device and app details. The developer does not receive or store this information. To limit personalized ads, delete or reset your advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads on Android. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Contact

If you email cs@footer.kr, your reply address and the information you provide are used to answer your request. Please do not send sensitive information. Contact the same address to request access to or deletion of your inquiry.

5. Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date if features or information-handling practices change.
